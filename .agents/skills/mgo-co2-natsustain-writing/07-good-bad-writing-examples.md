# Good and bad writing examples

## Example 1: Avoid overclaiming

Bad:
This material shows excellent CO2 capture performance and has great industrial application prospects.

Better:
The molten salt-modified MgO sorbent exhibits enhanced intermediate-temperature CO2 uptake under the tested conditions, although long-term cycling and process-level validation are still required before assessing industrial relevance.

Reason:
The revised sentence is more evidence-bounded and avoids unsupported industrial claims.

## Example 2: Mechanistic caution

Bad:
The molten salt proves the formation of Na2Mg(CO3)2.

Better:
The emergence of carbonate-related diffraction features during CO2 exposure is consistent with the formation of intermediate carbonate species, although definitive phase assignment requires complementary spectroscopic or crystallographic evidence.

Reason:
The revised sentence separates observation from phase assignment.
