# Project background and research gap

## Research topic
Molten salt-modified hierarchical porous MgO sorbents for intermediate-temperature CO2 capture.

## Core problem
Pristine MgO has theoretical potential for CO2 capture, but its practical carbonation is limited by sluggish kinetics, carbonate-layer passivation, and poor cyclic stability under intermediate-temperature conditions.

## Design concept
This project uses NaNO3–NaNO2–Li2CO3 molten salts to modify hierarchical porous MgO, aiming to improve interfacial wetting, ion transport, and reversible carbonate formation.

## Key research gap
Most molten salt-modified MgO studies rely on empirical salt selection. This work aims to establish a rational molten-salt design logic based on melting behavior, viscosity, phase stability, ion transport, and MgO–CO2 reaction compatibility.

## Avoid overclaiming
