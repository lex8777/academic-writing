# Mechanism boundaries

## Supported claims
- Molten salts can improve MgO carbonation by enhancing interfacial wetting and ion transport.
- Hierarchical porous MgO provides structural space for molten-salt infiltration.
- TG data can quantify CO2 uptake, regeneration behavior, and cycling decay.
- In situ XRD can track phase evolution during activation, carbonation, and regeneration.
- SEM/EDS can support morphology and elemental distribution, but cannot alone prove ion transport.

## Claims requiring caution
- Specific formation of Na2Mg(CO3)2 or Li2Mg(CO3)2 requires direct phase evidence.
- Defect-rich MgO should be described cautiously unless supported by XRD peak broadening, XPS, TEM, Raman, or related evidence.
- Molten salt should not be described as the dominant CO2 storage phase unless salt-only controls support it.
- Industrial feasibility should not be claimed without long-term cycling, impurity tolerance, scale-up, or techno-economic analysis.

## Preferred cautious language
Use:
- suggests
- indicates
- is consistent with
- may arise from
- can be partly attributed to

Avoid:
- proves
- fully confirms
- demonstrates beyond doubt
- unprecedented
- industrially applicable
