# Terminology

## Preferred terms
- molten salt-modified MgO
- molten salt-promoted MgO sorbent
- hierarchical porous MgO
- intermediate-temperature CO2 capture
- molten-salt-assisted carbonation
- carbonate-mediated ion transport
- interfacial carbonation
- cyclic stability
- thermal regeneration
- phase evolution
- reversible carbonate formation

## Use carefully
- adsorption: use only when discussing surface adsorption.
- carbonation: use when MgO reacts with CO2 to form carbonate species.
- sorbent: preferred general term for MgO-based CO2 capture materials.
- absorbent: avoid unless discussing bulk liquid absorption.

## Avoid
- excellent performance
- obvious mechanism
- breakthrough material
- revolutionary
- industrially ready
- perfect stability
