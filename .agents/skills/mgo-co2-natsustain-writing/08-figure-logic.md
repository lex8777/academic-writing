# Figure logic for molten salt-modified MgO CO2 capture

## Figure design principles
- Emphasize mechanism rather than instruments.
- Show hierarchical porous MgO as the structural host.
- Show molten salts as a continuous or semi-continuous ion-transport medium.
- Show CO2 transport, interfacial carbonation, carbonate formation, and regeneration.
- Avoid claiming a phase unless supported by XRD, Raman, FTIR/DRIFTS, or thermodynamic evidence.

## Suggested figure panels
- Panel a: rational molten-salt screening logic
- Panel b: synthesis of hierarchical porous MgO
- Panel c: molten-salt infiltration into porous MgO
- Panel d: CO2 capture and carbonate-mediated transport
- Panel e: in situ phase evolution during activation/capture/regeneration
- Panel f: cyclic stability and structural degradation mechanism

## Visual style
- Use restrained colors.
- Avoid unnecessary instrument icons.
- Prefer layered schematic, phase evolution arrows, and evidence-linked labels.
