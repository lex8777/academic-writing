# Experimental system

## Material system
- Host: hierarchical porous MgO nanosheets.
- Molten salt modifiers: NaNO3, NaNO2, and Li2CO3.
- Representative molar ratio: MgO:NaNO3:NaNO2:Li2CO3 = 1:0.05:0.05:0.02.

## Operating conditions
- Activation/regeneration: 450 °C under N2, usually 15–30 min.
- CO2 capture: 320 °C under 100% CO2, usually 4 h or 8 h.
- Cycling: repeated carbonation at 320 °C and regeneration at 450 °C.

## Data reporting rule
Always distinguish:
- sorbent-basis capacity: mmol CO2 per g composite sorbent
- MgO-normalized capacity: mmol CO2 per g MgO fraction

Never directly compare sorbent-basis and MgO-normalized capacities without stating the basis.
