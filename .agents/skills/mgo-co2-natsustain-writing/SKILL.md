# MgO molten-salt CO2 capture writing skill

Use this skill together with `nature-polishing` when preparing manuscript text for Nature Sustainability or related high-level sustainability and materials journals.

## Core task

Support academic writing, logical restructuring, claim checking, terminology consistency, mechanism clarification, and evidence-bounded polishing for manuscripts on molten salt-modified MgO sorbents for intermediate-temperature CO2 capture.

## Required behavior

1. Preserve the author's original scientific logic, data, sample names, temperatures, gas atmospheres, cycle numbers, and figure references.
2. Do not invent new data, new mechanisms, new citations, or unsupported sustainability implications.
3. Distinguish confirmed experimental evidence from mechanistic interpretation.
4. Keep claims restrained and evidence-based.
5. Avoid promotional words unless quantitatively justified.
6. Always check whether the statement is supported by TG, in situ XRD, XPS, SEM/EDS, Raman, DRIFTS, kinetic modelling, thermodynamic calculation, or literature evidence.
7. Distinguish sorbent-basis capacity from MgO-normalized capacity.
8. For Nature Sustainability, connect materials design to sustainability relevance without overstating industrial maturity.
